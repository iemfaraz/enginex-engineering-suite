Baselines, Export & Reporting
Save contract baselines, generate printed project reports, export CSV and JSON, and use the S-Curve for SEC/client submissions. The complete reporting workflow.
  • 📸
    Save BL01 Before StartingAlways save the Contract Baseline (BL01) immediately after finalising the approved schedule — before any actual progress. This is your legal benchmark.
  • 💾
    Export JSON WeeklyExport your schedule as .json at least weekly as a backup. Store it on SharePoint or email it to the team. Browser localStorage can be cleared accidentally.
  • 📊
    CSV for Excel AnalysisExport CSV to Excel for advanced filtering, pivot tables and custom charts. Useful for weekly site reports when you need to format data differently.
  • 🖨️
    Print = Client ReportThe Print function generates a formatted PDF of your schedule and EVM metrics. This is your monthly progress report for SEC, ARAMCO or consultant review.
