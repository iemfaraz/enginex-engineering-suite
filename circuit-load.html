01 / CIRCUIT DETAILS

Define the circuit

Single phase · AC

Current uses circuit L-N voltage. This tool sums apparent loads conservatively and assumes one single-phase final circuit. It does not model three-phase balancing, harmonics, motor starting or protection selectivity.

02 / CONNECTED LOADS

What is connected?

Quantity = number of assemblies. Outlets/assembly = socket positions inside each assembly. A double socket has 2 outlets; a 4-socket floor box has 4. Data and telephone ports do not count as power sockets.

Starting values: sockets 200 VA per outlet; lighting 20 W at PF 0.90. These are editable example allowances, not fixed IEC/SBC requirements. For known equipment, enter its rated input and PF. Use “per assembly” if a floor box has one total assigned load.

    Calculation evidence

    Per-outlet VA = rating in VA, or rated input W ÷ PF. Row VA = assemblies × outlets/assembly × per-outlet VA. With an assembly rating, the outlet multiplier is 1.

    Connected current = ΣVA ÷ V. Demand VA = Σ(row VA × row demand % ÷ 100). Design current Ib = demand VA × (1 + spare % ÷ 100) ÷ V.

    Load check only. Ib ≤ In ≤ Iz is an initial overload-coordination check, not full approval. Verify protective-device operating characteristics, fault disconnection, voltage drop, RCD requirements, socket ratings, circuit division and conductor correction factors separately.

    IEC / SBC design basis

    SBC 401:2024 Chapter 31 includes maximum demand and diversity. IEC 60364 covers the assessment and safety of low-voltage installations. Socket demand depends on the intended connected appliances and project use. This worksheet does not claim a mandatory VA value for every socket or a universal permissible socket count per circuit.
